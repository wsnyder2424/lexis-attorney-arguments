Motion
Defendant's Motion to Dismiss for Failure to State a Claim Under 35 U.S.C. § 101
TidalWave Networks, LLC v. Vellum Semiconductor, Inc.  |  S.D. Cal.  |  March 4, 2024  |  3:23-cv-02841-BAS-DEB
Intellectual Property | Patent Law

The asserted claims of the '482 patent are directed to the abstract idea of collecting, analyzing, and displaying network performance data, and they recite nothing more than generic computer components performing routine functions. Under the two-step framework of Alice Corp. v. CLS Bank International, the claims are ineligible for patent protection under 35 U.S.C. § 101.

Intellectual Property | Claim Construction

Plaintiff's infringement theory requires reading the limitation "in real time" out of the asserted claims entirely. Motion
Defendant's Motion to Dismiss Trademark Infringement Claims
Marin Apparel Co. v. Crestline Outfitters, Inc.  |  S.D. Cal.  |  July 19, 2023  |  3:23-cv-00614-LAB-AGS
Intellectual Property | Trademark Law

The Sleekcraft factors weigh decisively against any finding of likelihood of confusion. The parties' marks differ in sight, sound, and meaning; the goods travel in distinct channels of trade; and Plaintiff has pleaded no facts suggesting actual confusion in the four years the marks have coexisted in the marketplace.

Opposition
Defendant's Opposition to Plaintiff's Motion for Preliminary Injunction
Sierra BioCore, Inc. v. Vellum Semiconductor, Inc.  |  S.D. Cal.  |  April 27, 2023  |  3:23-cv-00298-CAB-JLB
Intellectual Property | Remedies

Plaintiff cannot establish irreparable harm because the information it claims as a trade secret was disclosed in two of Plaintiff's own published patent applications more than two years before the alleged misappropriation. Public disclosure forecloses trade-secret protection as a matter of law.

Motion
Defendant's Motion to Dismiss Copyright Infringement Claims on Fair Use Grounds
Lattice Audio LLC v. Northbeam Studios, Inc.  |  S.D. Cal.  |  January 23, 2023  |  3:22-cv-01443-JLS-MSB
Intellectual Property | Copyright Law

Defendant's use of short audio excerpts is transformative under the framework articulated in Andy Warhol Foundation v. Goldsmith. The excerpts serve a different expressive purpose, occupy a fraction of the original works, and do not function as a market substitute for Plaintiff's licensing program.

Motion
Defendant's Motion to Exclude Plaintiff's Damages Expert Under Daubert

Plaintiff's expert applies the entire market value rule to a multi-component product without the predicate showing that the patented feature drives consumer demand.
